How to Play

Basics

Type the falling words to destroy them before they reach the bottom.

Destroying a special word triggers its semantic meaning as a game effect.

Word Colors

GREEN = helpful (heal, shield, slow…)

RED = harmful (fast, scramble, leech…)

YELLOW = chaotic / neutral

BLUE / CYAN / etc. = regular words (by tier)

Combos & Strategy

Chain kills quickly for a score MULTIPLIER (x1.5 → x2 → x3)

Kill words near the TOP for +25% score. Bottom words give −25%.

Destroy words within 1 second of each other for a QUICK bonus!

Boss Words

Every 4 levels a BOSS word appears — type it multiple times to defeat it!

Controls

TYPE = destroy words  |  ESC = pause

Same word x2? Type once, destroy ALL copies!
